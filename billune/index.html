Privacy Policy

Effective: November 20, 2026 · Developer: Yusuf Fırat Gül, Istanbul, Türkiye · Contact: yusuff.gull@icloud.com

Billune is an offline-first subscription and bill tracker. The short version: Billune has no server of its own and does not collect personal data or track you. The only thing that ever leaves your device is a purchase handled by Apple, if you choose to buy Billune Premium.

There is no account

Billune has no sign-up, login, or user profile.

What stays on your device

Your subscriptions, bills, price history, notes and settings are stored in a local database on your iPhone or iPad. This data never leaves your device, is not backed up to any cloud by Billune, and is permanently deleted when you delete the app.

You can export your own data to a CSV file from Settings (Premium). The file is created on your device and shared only where you choose to send it.

Notifications

Renewal reminders are scheduled and delivered locally on your device. No notification content is sent to any server.

Purchases

Billune Premium is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. Billune uses no third-party payment or purchase service and never sees your card details, name, email or Apple ID; it only asks the App Store whether your account owns Premium, to unlock and restore it. Apple handles billing, receipts and refunds.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • We don't ask for your name, email, photos, contacts or location.
  • We don't sell data to anyone.

Children

Billune is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update is posted here with a new effective date.

Support

Questions, bug reports or feedback? The address below reaches the developer directly, usually with a reply within a day or two.

yusuff.gull@icloud.com

Restoring Premium

Reinstalled the app or switched devices? Open Settings and tap Restore purchases (it is also on the Premium screen). Premium tied to your Apple ID is re-applied at no extra charge.

I'm not getting reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for Billune in iOS Settings → Notifications. Reminders are local, so they work without an internet connection.

Moving my data to a new device

Because your data stays on the device, Billune does not sync between devices. With Premium you can export a CSV from Settings to keep a copy.
