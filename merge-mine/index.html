Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026 · Developer: Yusuf Fırat Gül, Istanbul, Türkiye

Merge Mine is a single-player puzzle game. This page explains what happens to your data when you play it.

What the game itself stores

Your progress — coins, unlocked mine upgrades, your best tier reached and your daily reward streak — is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else. If you delete the app, that progress is gone; we have no copy of it and no way to recover it.

Advertising

Merge Mine shows ads through Google AdMob to keep the game free. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), a consent message is shown on first launch before any ads are requested. On iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether Merge Mine may track your activity across other apps and websites. Choosing “Ask App Not to Track” is fully supported and the game plays identically; you will simply see less personalized ads.

AdMob's data handling is governed by Google's own policy, since we never see or store what it collects: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Remove Ads and Rescue Pack purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment details, only a confirmation that a purchase succeeded, which unlocks the feature on your device.

Children's privacy

Merge Mine is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date above is updated and the change is posted here before it takes effect.

How does merging work?

Tap any empty slot in the grid to place the next tile shown above the board. If it lands next to a tile of the same tier, they merge into the next one up: Stone → Copper → Iron → Silver → Gold → Diamond. One placement can trigger several merges in a row.

My mine filled up — is that it?

When every slot is taken, that run ends. Watch a rewarded ad or use a Rescue Pack to clear a few low-tier tiles and reopen the board, or start a fresh mine. Nothing you have unlocked or earned is lost either way.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads, or lost it after reinstalling

Open Settings from the home screen and tap Restore Purchases. This re-links any past purchase to your device using your Apple ID, no need to buy again.

Can I turn off sound?

Yes, Settings on the home screen has a sound effects toggle.

Something else

Email the address above with your device model and iOS version if it is a bug report. That is usually all that is needed to reproduce it.
